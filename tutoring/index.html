The approach

Support without
the cost.

While studying for the MCAT, the content you need to master can feel insurmountable, and the cost can add up quickly. MCAT tutors can range from $35–$300 per hour, putting that support out of reach for many students.

As a Pell Grant recipient, I feel strongly about equal access to resources and educational opportunity regardless of economic status. I’m excited to offer my advice and knowledge to those who need it at no cost!

What I offer

01

One-on-one meetings

We’ll discuss personalized study schedules, content gaps, and passage strategies. I recommend 1–2 hours per week, depending on your availability.

02

Advice from my MCAT experience

Get advice on mastering content, which resources are worth your time and money, and how to make the most of your studying each day.

How it works
